# NYC 2026 Field Guide

**[Open the travel guide](https://johnmagnuson19.github.io/NYC1/)**

https://johnmagnuson19.github.io/NYC1/

A travel guide to the 30 places on the NYC 2026 map, sorted by type. Every stop has a short note, a practical tip, and quick links to Google Maps and to photos. It works on a phone, so you can pull it up while you're out.

---

## How to use it

1. Open the link above on your phone or computer.
2. Tap a category in the bar at the top to see only that type of place. Tap **All** to see everything again.
3. On any card, tap **Map** for directions or **Photos** to see what the place looks like.
4. Scroll to the bottom for **Easy pairings**, groups of nearby stops that make a good half-day.

Tip: add the page to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu → Add to Home screen.

## Good to know before you go

- **The Met is closed on Wednesdays.**
- **B&H is closed on Saturdays** and on Jewish holidays, and closes early on Fridays.
- **Aperture** just opened its new home at 380 Columbus Ave. It's free and open Wednesday through Sunday.
- **Call ahead** for Casey Rubber Stamps, Joanne Hendricks Cookbooks and George Glazer Gallery. Their hours are limited or irregular.
- **Birdland:** reserve ahead, and expect a music charge plus a food or drink minimum.

Hours change, so check each place before you go.
