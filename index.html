Easy pairings

stops that go well together

  1. Central Park south to north. Enter at 72nd St, walk the Mall to the Beethoven bust, down the steps to Bethesda Terrace, then up past the Ramble to Belvedere Castle. Exit east to the Met, and finish with an egg cream at Lexington Candy Shop.
  2. Museum Mile. The Met and the Guggenheim are six blocks apart on Fifth Ave. George Glazer Gallery is a short walk east on 94th St after the Guggenheim.
  3. Lower East Side photo afternoon. ICP Museum and AUTOPHOTO are two blocks apart around Essex, Orchard and Delancey.
  4. East Village small shops. Casey Rubber Stamps and Limited to One are around the corner from each other; John Derian is a 10-minute walk south on 2nd St.
  5. West Village wander. Dauphinette and Pink Olive are a few blocks apart; Joanne Hendricks Cookbooks is a walk south into Hudson Square.
  6. West Village dinner. Don Angie and Graciela are a block apart on Greenwich Ave, with Bangkok Supper Club a few minutes west. John's of Bleecker Street is the easy no-reservation fallback.
  7. Midtown West records and gear. Jazz Record Center (26th St), B&H (34th St), then Rough Trade at Rockefeller Center and a late set at Birdland on 44th St.
  8. Brooklyn day. Dumbo in the morning for the Manhattan Bridge view, Green-Wood Cemetery and Prospect Park in the afternoon. Dinner at Theodora in Fort Greene, then end the day at Kissa Kissa in Crown Heights, a short ride from Prospect Park. Brooklyn Film Camera is in East Williamsburg, a separate trip on the L train.
  9. Records by night. Hunt for LPs at Limited to One or Jazz Record Center in the afternoon, then hear them properly at a listening bar: All Blues in Tribeca, or Eavesdrop in Greenpoint, with tacos first at Taqueria Ramirez around the corner.
